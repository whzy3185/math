# Literature / novelty mode

Use for paper mining, background, open-status and prior-art work.

1. Choose Seed 0 from the supplied paper/current theorem; for new topics choose a recent theorem-driven seed.
2. Identify S0 original source, S1 closest theorem, S2 recent frontier, S4 highest novelty risk.
3. Search exact theorem language, synonym/notation variants, strengthening words, method analogues, backward citations, forward citations and same-author follow-ups.
4. Compare theorem-to-theorem: domain, hypotheses, conclusion, sharpness/equality, global vs bounded, method, relation = same/stronger/weaker/incomparable.
5. Never certify “open” from an old paper alone; date the audit and check later versions/work.
6. Stop the first search round once source, closest theorem, frontier, terminology, priority risk and one testable target are clear.
7. Reopen literature after a material theorem upgrade or before publication freeze.

Background narrative: classical problem → strongest refinement → surviving obstruction → current gap → present theorem. Literature must change the theorem/proof strategy, not merely enlarge the bibliography.
