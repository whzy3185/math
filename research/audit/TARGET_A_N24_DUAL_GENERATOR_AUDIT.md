## Generator Lemma

The FKM recursion maintains the invariant that the prefix is the
lexicographically least prefix among its rotations and that `period` is the
least candidate rotational period. At a leaf, `period|n` is therefore
equivalent to a complete necklace representative, and every necklace has one
such leaf. The fixed-weight bounds prune only prefixes whose current number of
ones already exceeds the requested weight or cannot reach it with the
remaining positions; hence they do not remove a valid leaf.

For a necklace of rotational orbit size `t`, reverse its `n` bits and minimize
over its `t` rotations. If the original code is larger, its reflection pair
has already been or will be represented by the smaller code and is omitted.
If the codes coincide, reflection lies in the rotational orbit and the
dihedral orbit size is `t`; otherwise the two rotational orbits are disjoint
and the size is `2t`. This proves the reflection filter and reported orbit
size.

The record-for-record `n=24` comparison is stronger than aggregate Burnside
agreement and rules out compensating omissions and duplications at the first
production order.
