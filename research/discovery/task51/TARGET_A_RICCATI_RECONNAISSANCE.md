# Target A Riccati Reconnaissance

The proved local `A^2` formula makes period-eight cell grouping block banded,
but the smallest natural blocks still couple across more than one scalar cell.
Numerical fixed-point reconnaissance did not produce a simple rational
Loewner interval simultaneously compatible with G6, G10, gap2, and both
holonomies at `beta<8`.

The route was stopped at Level 1 because the exact recurrence reduction had
already reached dimension four and supplied an exact finite prefix.  No claims
`L<=S<=U`, `F(L)>=L`, or `F(U)<=U` are made.

Status: `WEAK`; a defect-specific block Riccati proof remains possible but is
not currently competitive with dominant-mode recurrence analysis.
