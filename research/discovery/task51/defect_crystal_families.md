# Defect-Crystal Families

The required motifs give the following deterministic Bloch values:

| Motif | Legal cell | `R^2` | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `[4]` | 8 | 7.804226065181 | period-eight ground phase |
| `[4,6]` | 10 | 7.916381551745 | proved period-ten family |
| `[4,8]` | 12 | 7.935544897091 | promising |
| `[4,10]` | 14 | 7.963470219460 | promising |
| `[4,6,4,6]` | 20 | 7.916381551745 | repetition of period ten |
| `[4,4,6]` | 28 | 7.967996577385 | sub-eight mixed crystal |
| `[4,6,6]` | 32 | 8.066474002665 | falsified as sub-eight |

The last comparison shows that defect density alone does not order phases;
the local arrangement of equal charges matters.
