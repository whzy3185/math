# Target A Chiral and Topological Audit

The existing exact period-eight construction has `tau_(i+4)=-tau_i`.  On a
Bloch fiber, the normalized operator `J_z=xi^-1 D T4(z)`, `xi^2=z`, satisfies
`J_z^2=I` and anticommutes with `H(z)`, producing the exact `4+4` chiral block
reduction.

This grading depends on the square-root choice of the Bloch coordinate.  A
globally fixed, cell-independent off-diagonal block `B(z)` with an invariant
`winding(det B)` was not obtained.  More importantly, G6, G10, and gap2 join
translated sectors of the same period-eight bulk.  No bulk invariant difference
was found that distinguishes the two sides or predicts the observed localized
levels.

Berry/Zak quantities change with unit-cell origin in this comparison and do
not provide a protected interface count.  The localized levels are therefore
best understood spectrally through matching, not as topologically protected
zero modes.

Result: `NO_TOPOLOGICAL_PROTECTION_SIGNAL`.  It should not enter the final
paper story beyond the already useful chiral bulk reduction.
