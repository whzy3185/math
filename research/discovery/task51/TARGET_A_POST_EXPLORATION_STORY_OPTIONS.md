# Target A Post-Exploration Story Options

## Story A: Eventual all-even counterexamples

Main theorem: a uniform finite-ring cap plus threshold crossing.  Ingredients:
exact G6/G10 interfaces, exact recurrence reduction, exact finite prefix.
Missing: dominant-mode tail and two-interface bound.  Strength 4/5; JCTB fit
4/5; computation moderate.

## Story B: Elementary charged phase slips

Main theorem: charge conservation, exact G6 excitation, and fixed-r multi-slip
upper bounds for all even residues.  Ingredients: charge law, G6 theorem,
three-G6 reframe, exact c6 polynomial.  Missing: fixed-r lower/counting theory.
Strength 5/5; JCTB fit 5/5; computation moderate.

## Story C: Unique periodic ground state

Main theorem: period eight uniquely minimizes every legal periodic phase.
Ingredients: exact p<=24 frontier and M4-M6.  Missing: arbitrary-period local
certificate; current window relaxation leaves 30 bounded cycles.  Strength
5/5; JCTB fit 5/5; proof proximity 2/5.

## Story D: Ground state, elementary defects, and asymptotic m_n

Main theorem: period-eight bulk plus common nonzero residue limit c6 and an
effective multi-slip expansion.  Ingredients: all central Task 51 discoveries.
Missing: global lower bounds and high-precision interaction theorem.  Strength
5/5; JCTB fit 5/5; proof proximity 3/5.

## Story E: Commensurate defect crystals

Main theorem: exact period8/10/12/14 branches and density crossings.  Ingredients:
13-phase atlas and exact period8/10 families.  Missing: exact upper bounds for
new phases and a phase-envelope theorem.  Strength 4/5; JCTB fit 4/5.

**Primary recommendation:** Story B.  It has a proved elementary constant,
exact charge bookkeeping, a decisive G10 reframe, and a bounded next theorem.

**Fallback:** Story A.  The recurrence route is now sharply reduced and can
recover the existing counterexample narrative if multi-slip lower bounds prove
too expensive.

The formal manuscript remains frozen.
