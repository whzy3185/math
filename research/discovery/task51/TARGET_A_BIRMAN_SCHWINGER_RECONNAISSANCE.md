# Target A Birman-Schwinger Reconnaissance

A charged interface is not a finite-rank perturbation of one globally fixed
period-eight gauge: it joins translated bulk sectors.  A direct
`A_defect^2-A_bulk^2` Birman-Schwinger model is therefore ill-posed until a
piecewise bulk unitary is supplied.

Neutral compact combinations are finite rank.  Exact integer rank and exact
Sturm inertia give:

| Core | Rank | Positive | Negative |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| G6 + gap2 | 8 | 4 | 4 |
| G10 + three gap2 | 16 | 8 | 8 |
| gap8 + two gap2 | 12 | 6 | 6 |
| two G6 + two gap2 | 14 | 7 | 7 |

Thus simple positive-rank interlacing cannot by itself prove that only one
upper-gap state exists.  Infinite and finite Green matrices and a method-of-
images estimate were not derived after this obstruction appeared.
