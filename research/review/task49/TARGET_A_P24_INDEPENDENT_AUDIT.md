# Target A p<=24 Independent Audit

## Scope and Independence

The second implementation independently supplies binary-word generation,
legality filtering, rotations, reflections, canonical representatives, orbit
multiplicity, the `Q -> tau` lift, primitive-period detection, both holonomy
matrices, closed-walk moments, and exact integer Rayleigh verification.  It
does not import the production canonicalization or moment helpers.  Existing
artifacts are inputs only for the target partition and certificate vectors;
all accepted rational inequalities are recomputed.

During development the independent route exposed an implicit even-period
assumption in its first legality filter.  The correct condition is
`sum(bits) mod 2 = p mod 2`, equivalent to `product(Q)=1`.  After correction,
odd and even displayed periods are generated by the same stated rule.  This
incident is retained as evidence that the audit was behaviorally independent.
