# Task 50 Signed-Graph/Combinatorial Review

## Findings

### MAJOR 1: explicit residue coverage is not yet eventual coverage

The gap words are legal and have the claimed residue formulas.  However,
without a uniform spectral-radius estimate their existence does not prove a
counterexample at every sufficiently large order.  The future statement must
not replace this analytic hypothesis by the Task 49 finite crossing atlas.

### MODERATE 1: the second G6 defect orientation remains part of Gate 5

The two-interface transfer must retain the oriented second defect and twisted
closure.  Treating both defects as an identical scalar scatterer would erase
the geometry and holonomy information relevant to the mod16 preference.

## Accepted Components

The conventions `Q_i=tau_i tau_{i+1}`, `tau_0=1`, and the left/right defect
lattices determine the G6 and G10 words without ambiguity.  The single-family
words contain `2k` defects.  The symmetric and shifted two-G6 words have the
correct total orders and even defect counts.  The period-8 residue-zero family
remains an existing proved dependency.
