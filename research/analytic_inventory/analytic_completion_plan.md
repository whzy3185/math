This plan strengthens only the frozen period-eight article.  It does not
reopen the R2/R4/R6/G6 or all-even-classification programmes.  Lean work
begins only after all four work packages below are frozen.

| Work package | Deliverable | Completion criterion | Status |

| A | Self-contained twisted-benchmark Fourier lemma | explicit \(2\times2\) Fourier block, correct grid maximization, and an independent symbolic audit | complete |

## Execution order

1. Complete A and replace every use of the inherited twisted formula by the
   new lemma.
2. Audit B's indices and recurrences independently of existing generators.
3. Compress C into one theorem and two corollaries suitable for a main-text
   section.
4. Run D as a theorem-dependency and wording audit, then freeze the English
   article blueprint.

## Non-negotiable evidence rule

A finite integer recurrence may remain in B or C only if the recurrence,
initial data, exact values used, and its logical implication are all printed.
No floating-point spectrum, signing search, stored certificate, or unresolved
Riccati assertion may enter the article theorem chain.
