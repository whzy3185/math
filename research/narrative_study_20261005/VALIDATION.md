# Study validation and handoff

## Checked

- Seven version-pinned arXiv primary-source PDFs were downloaded and inspected beyond their abstracts
- All first-submission dates lie within 2023-10-05 through 2026-10-05; every studied version predates the cutoff
- Author names and qualifying affiliations were checked on the selected PDF title pages; affiliations are version-specific
- Page and section locators in the reading notes refer to the selected PDFs, not potentially different journal layouts
- Five source figure pages were visually inspected; their page numbers are recorded in source_catalog.json
- Each reading note contains fewer than 200 whitespace-delimited words including its heading and source link; no quotations are reproduced
- Source observations were written before the manuscript-specific application pass
- The three current manuscripts were read without consulting the isolated review directories
- Original manuscript files were not edited; baseline TeX hashes are recorded in input_snapshots.json
- The Domination and Mycielski revision editors received the rubric and application plan
- Publication contents are allowlisted; source PDFs, full-text extraction, rendered source images, and working scripts are excluded

## Limits

This is a purposive narrative sample, not a systematic review, nationality classification, representative survey, citation-quality ranking, or proof audit. S5 is retained as a same-team contrast case; it is not a model of all-round prose quality. The arXiv record is used for date eligibility, and the S6 title-page date discrepancy is explicitly recorded. None of the sampled papers supplies novelty or correctness evidence for the three manuscripts merely by resemblance.

The manuscript-specific application advice is an independent editorial judgment. The study did not rerun the manuscripts' graph enumerations, rational certificate programs, or Lean builds. A reported Mycielski endpoint example from another editor is flagged as unverified here. Mathematical changes require their own evidence and review.

## Remaining integration

The assigned editors must implement selected changes in new manuscript copies, compile and inspect those versions, and provide before/after evidence. A publisher must place the allowlisted study files and the authorized manuscript revisions on the user's new repository branch. This study does not claim those downstream steps have happened.

The sampled papers should not be added to a mathematical bibliography merely because they influenced exposition. Cite a paper in a manuscript only when its mathematical content or a specifically discussed result is relevant; the writing study already records the editorial sources.
