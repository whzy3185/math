# S6 Fractional balanced chromatic number and arboricity of planar (signed) graphs

Source: [2505.16808v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.16808v1). Page references are printed PDF pages.

Abstract: define two related parameters → counterexample to a named conjecture → quantitative amplification (p. 1). Introduction roles: historical gadget, target conjecture, comparison of parameters, then a simple-first proof plan (§1, pp. 1–3). Theorem 6 first establishes the qualitative obstruction; later theorems quantify it (§§2–5).

Figures show successive gadget assembly, including the final constructions (pp. 4–7). Proof lemmas isolate the property each gadget enforces (pp. 3–6). Exact coloring tables provide checkable upper witnesses alongside lower arguments (pp. 10–11, 14–15).

Calibration is especially useful: the limiting value is not advertised as an attained finite example (p. 9); later discussion identifies the ceiling of this particular construction method (pp. 10–11, 15). Transfer: explain an obstruction before optimizing constants, and distinguish a finite certificate, a family limit, and a universal extremal value. Our Mycielski census should illustrate and delimit a theorem, never substitute for its general proof or certify novelty.
