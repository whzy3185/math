# S1 Graph operations and a unified method for kinds of Turán-type problems on paths, cycles and matchings

Source: [2312.08226v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.08226v2). Page references are printed PDF pages.

Abstract: definitions → three structural theorems → applications (p. 1). Introduction roles: separate literatures (§§1.1–1.3), missing parameter ranges and explicit Problems 1–2 (p. 4), then common abstraction (§2, pp. 5–6). The theorem hierarchy is framework → parameter verification (§3) → consequences (§4) → proofs (§5). This makes generality demonstrable rather than adjectival.

Mechanism: monotone operations produce a structured graph; the main proof introduces intermediate claims (pp. 14, 17). The outline is navigational, not a full explanation of the proof difficulty (§1.5). The conclusion supplies a numerical counterexample to an apparently convenient reduction and states unresolved extensions (§6, p. 23). No figure is needed for that counterexample.

Calibration: weak feasibility gives an extremal value without classifying every optimizer (p. 6). That distinction is more useful to imitate than the broad priority language on p. 4. For our manuscripts, require a separate scope sentence whenever an exact formula, equality classification, or general framework is claimed. Do not inflate a single-family calculation into unification.
