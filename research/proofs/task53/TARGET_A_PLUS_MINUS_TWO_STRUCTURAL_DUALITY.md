# Plus/Minus-two Structural Relation

Exact transfer construction gives unsquared symmetric Evans cores for gap2
and gap6. Task 53 tested lambda sign reversal and reciprocal `P` exchange,
with exact scalar normalization. None maps one core to the other.

After substituting the reciprocal stable-branch relations, separating even
and odd powers of `lambda`, squaring, and eliminating `P`, the two
resultants are exactly equal over `Q[y]`, including every factor and
multiplicity. This explains the common degree-ten polynomial at the level of
the projected elimination curve only.

It does not prove an unsquared conjugacy, a physical branch exchange, or
equality of the two interface spectra. Indeed the degree-ten roots near
`7.905369` and `8.080986` are selected by different physical matching
problems.
