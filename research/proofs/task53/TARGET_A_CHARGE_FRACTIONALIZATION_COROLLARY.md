# Charge Fractionalization Corollary

Task 52 proves the exact isolated-interface comparisons

```text
c_(+4)>c6,
c_(+6)=c10>c6.
```

The two-G6 and three-G6 balanced ring constructions have total charges
`+4` and `+6`. Their spectral-radius squares are at most
`c6+o(1)` by the fixed-r global cap. Since both displayed isolated levels are
strictly separated from `c6`, sufficiently large interface separation gives

```text
rho(two separated +2 slips)^2<c_(+4),
rho(three separated +2 slips)^2<c_(+6)=c10.
```

Thus these larger charges admit asymptotic constructions whose elementary
`+2` constituents are cheaper than the corresponding isolated composite
interface. This is a comparison of explicit constructions. It is not a
minimization theorem over every decomposition of a fixed charge.
