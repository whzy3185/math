# Feshbach Effective Matrix: Task 53 Boundary

The natural low-dimensional space is spanned by the `r` truncated G6 modes.
Task 52 proves that its Gram matrix is `I+O((9/25)^L)` and that the residual
of each basis vector is `O((9/25)^L)`. Formally this gives

```text
H_eff=c6 I_r+T+R.
```

Task 53 does not assert a Feshbach theorem because uniform invertibility of
the complementary block on a fixed contour is the unresolved D1 input.
Accordingly no exact rank, leading coupling coefficient, or holonomy formula
is inferred from the numerical clusters.
