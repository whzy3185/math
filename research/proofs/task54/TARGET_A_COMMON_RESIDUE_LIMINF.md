# Common-Residue Liminf Status

For any pointed local limit of a sequence of finite rings,

```text
||H_infinity|| <= liminf_j rho(A_j)^2.
```

Consequently a contradiction sequence uniformly below `c6` is impossible
whenever one can choose roots whose pointed limit has spectral top at least
`c6`.  This proves the lower bound for sequences containing G6 neighborhoods
whose pure-bulk radii tend to infinity: truncated G6 eigenvectors transfer to
the finite rings and force the liminf to be at least `c6`.

It does not prove the unrestricted statements

```text
liminf_(k->infinity) m_(8k+r)^2 >= c6,  r=2,4,6.
```

The residue charge can escape every fixed window, and an arbitrary pointed
limit may be periodic bulk, a non-G6 tight cluster, a multi-component or
aperiodic operator.  No existing theorem forces one such limit to have edge
at least `c6`.

Status: restricted dilute-G6 liminf `PROVED`; unrestricted common-residue
liminf `OPEN`.
