# Optimized Global IMS Cap

Let a legal ring contain `r in {1,2,3}` G6 interfaces with minimum cyclic
site separation `D`. If `R>=4` and

```text
2(R+4)<D,
```

then every range-four enlargement of a radius-`R` tent support meets at most
one interface. Task 53's patch classification and global single-G6 edge
therefore apply to every localized quadratic form. Taking

```text
R=floor((D-9)/2)
```

gives

```text
rho(A_ring)^2
<=c6+(240R-342)/(R(2R^2+1))
<=c6+120/R^2.
```

The strict support condition also prevents cyclic tent self-collision in the
orders where this cap is used. Both holonomies and both G6 orientations are
inherited from the Task 53 local-model classification.
