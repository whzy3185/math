# Exact-r Riesz Theorem

Task 52 supplies at least `r` eigenvalues in an exponentially shrinking
neighborhood of `c6`. The Task 54 complement estimate shows, by the min-max
principle, that at most `r` eigenvalues can exceed `c6-1/200`.

For sufficiently large minimum separation the Task 52 cluster lies inside

```text
[c6-1/400,c6+1/400].
```

The interval is above the complementary spectral cap. It therefore contains
exactly `r` eigenvalues counted with multiplicity. Equivalently, a contour
surrounding this interval has a Riesz projection of rank `r`.

This does not assert that the `r` eigenvalues are simple.
