# c6 Polynomial Root Geometry

The irreducible degree-ten polynomial has exactly eight real roots and one
nonreal conjugate pair. Exact rational isolating intervals give the real
approximations

```text
0.0141689623069, 0.181121980768, 3.665857706348,
3.787911388433, 4.121833034741, 4.496243966215,
7.905369311620, 8.080985802104.
```

The seventh root is the G6 `q=+2` level and the eighth is the gap-2 `q=-2`
level, each selected by an independent interval Evans theorem. Task 52 gives
no physical interface interpretation to the six smaller real roots. The
polynomial discriminant is nonzero (and negative), so all ten roots are
simple.
