# Exact Gap-Six Defect Transfer

## Convention

On the infinite line put

\[
Q_i=+1
\iff
\bigl(i\le0\text{ and }i\equiv0\pmod4\bigr)
\text{ or }
\bigl(i\ge6\text{ and }i\equiv6\pmod4\bigr).
\]

Fix `tau_0=1` and impose `tau_{i+1}=Q_i tau_i`.  The left bulk cut is
`-8`, the right bulk cut is `14`, and

\[
P_6(\lambda)=T_{13}(\lambda)\cdots T_{-8}(\lambda).
\]

The left and right eight-step bulk monodromies are

\[
M_L=T_{-9}\cdots T_{-16},qquad
M_R=T_{21}\cdots T_{14}.
\]

The cuts lie in exact period-eight bulk.  Changing either cut by a complete
cell multiplies a matching basis by an invertible bulk monodromy and therefore
does not change the Evans zero set.

## Exactness

Every entry of `P6` is an integer polynomial in `lambda` of degree at most 11,
and

\[
\det P_6(\lambda)=1.
\]

The complete 4-by-4 polynomial matrix, the triangle-flux word from indices
`-10` through `15`, the cut, and the orientation convention are stored in
`certificates/g6_defect_transfer.json`.  They are generated by direct
multiplication in `Z[lambda]`; no fitted coefficient occurs.
