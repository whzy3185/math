# Coordinate the full paper workflow

Use WORKFLOW.md and the record templates. Keep separate the mathematical claim ledger, administrative review registry, and neutral packets. Assign a distinct reviewer and editor. Create new blind review contexts with no inherited conversation or reports, and cap every review context at two rounds. Log exposures and retire/replace contaminated contexts; never shop for a favorable verdict by hiding substantive objections.

Before target-specific drafting, read the chosen journal/publisher's current AI and authorship policies and compare them to actual tool use. Keep technical quality, policy eligibility and authorization to publish/submit as distinct decisions. Continue useful mathematical research while a venue or permission decision is pending.

Use the validator for identity, structure, context and gate checks only. Require evidence for each gate and make explicit what it cannot check. Freeze a new version after every material edit; invalidate changed claims and their dependents. Do not mark an issue closed because an editor says “fixed.”

Stop a paper's iteration when the defined technical and policy requirements are met, or the exact remaining blocker requires research, external access or a human decision. Deliver the current frozen candidate and next action. This workflow never authorizes journal submission, public sharing, author invention, or a false AI-use declaration.
