# Target A Two-Interface and mod-16 Reconnaissance

All dihedral-inequivalent separations of two gap-6 slips were scanned for both
holonomies at orders 52 through 156, covering both residues 4 and 12 modulo
16.  The two leading positive interface levels split around the single-slip
level and converge exponentially as the slips separate.

For orders congruent to 4 modulo 16, the minimum is the symmetric split.  For
orders congruent to 12 modulo 16, the minimum moves one period-8 bulk cell
away from the symmetric split.  At the orders where the splitting is clearly
resolved, `alpha=-1` gives the minimum.  At very large separation the two
holonomies become indistinguishable at double precision, as expected from
exponentially weak interaction.

Thus the earlier absence of the 12-mod-16 sequence was a seed-placement
effect, not a spectral obstruction.  The evidence supports interface-state
hybridization, parity-dependent separation, and finite-ring holonomy as the
mechanism.

Overall: `MOD16_INTERFACE_SIGNAL = STRONG`.
