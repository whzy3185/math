# Target A Residue 12 mod 16 Reconnaissance

The structured scan covered all prescribed orders from 44 through 508 and
both holonomies.  It compared a single gap 8, every inequivalent two-gap-6
separation, a gap-10/gap-2 pair, and a fixed four-slip charge decomposition.

The winning stable family is two gap-6 slips with `alpha=-1`, separated one
period-8 bulk cell away from the symmetric split.  Order 44 is not a
counterexample.  Every prescribed order from 60 through 508 is a numerical
counterexample and all 29 candidates have exact rational upper/threshold
sandwich certificates using sparse rational LDL positivity.

- First exact order: `60`
- Exact candidates: `29/29`
- Asymptotic level: `c6 = 7.905369311620327... < 8`
- Classification: `RESIDUE12_STABLE_C_LT_8_FAMILY`

Together with the already known structural families in the other even
residue classes, this makes eventual failure at every even order a serious
proof target.  It is not yet a theorem because uniform analytic bounds for
the finite families have not been proved.
