# Target A Hankel Beyond-p24 Stress Test

For each displayed period 25 through 40, five deterministic adversarial
candidates were selected: a target truncation, a low-defect word, a gap-4-like
word, a long-period construction, and a seeded control.  Legality is enforced
before exact moments and Hankel principal minors through depth five are
evaluated.

- Periods: 25--40
- States: 80
- Survivors through depth five: 8
- Classification: `STRONG`
- Evidence status: `SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE_ONLY`

This result is not an exhaustive classification, not an arbitrary-period
theorem, and not evidence that all difficult states were sampled.  Its only
role is to stress the bounded-period rigidity mechanism beyond the proved
frontier without starting a generic high-period search.
