# Two-Defect Geometry Anomalies

## Maximal Separation Is Not the Governing Rule

Only `p=8` is minimized at the maximal separation `p/2`. For every tested even
period `p=10,...,64`, the numerical minimum occurs at separation `s=4`.
Consequently the proposed maximal-separation principle is false in this
family, and simple monotone decrease with separation is also false.

## Other Sub-Eight Phases

The adaptive numerical estimates are below 8 at `(p,s)=(10,4),(12,4),
(14,4),(16,4)` in addition to the target `(8,4)`. Each of the four non-target
cases has an exact endpoint Rayleigh certificate proving `R(Q)^2>eta`.
Therefore these are structurally interesting sub-eight numerical phases, but
none threatens the target value.

## New Structural Signal

The conspicuous fixed separation `s=4`, rather than normalized or antipodal
separation, is the main signal. Its best value rises from `eta` at `p=8`,
remains numerically below 8 through `p=16`, and approaches a value above 8 as
the remote arc grows. This suggests a local four-step defect interaction with
a finite-size return-path correction.
