# Venue Architecture Audit

This report selects only papers that improve the present manuscript's
structure. It does not impose a quota on target journals.

## Journal of Graph Theory

### Lin--Ning (2021)

- Main classification is stated early.
- The large-order mechanism and finite exceptional closure have visibly
  different proof roles.
- The all-order conclusion is synthesized after both are established.
- Reusable lesson: present computation as finite completion of a theorem whose
  structural tail is already intelligible.

### Goedgebeur--Schaudt (2018)

- A structural reduction makes the computational domain complete before
  enumeration begins.
- Canonicalization is part of proof architecture, not an implementation
  appendix.
- Reusable lesson: state completeness contract before reporting counts.

## European Journal of Combinatorics

### Hu--Liu (2025)

- The introduction moves from signed graph functional to non-bipartite Cayley
  consequences through one named structural bridge.
- Main theorems are grouped by the graph classes to which the method applies.
- Reusable lesson: cycle squares may be described as a structured
  non-bipartite circulant family without claiming the Hu--Liu theorem solves
  the signing problem.

No second EJC exemplar was added: a lower-ranked signed-graph paper would add
topic breadth without improving the manuscript architecture.

## Linear Algebra and its Applications

### Korotyaev--Saburova (2023)

- Operator and fiber definitions precede formula-driven spectral conclusions.
- Flux/cycle data appear as coefficients of finite fiber quantities rather
  than as a detached graph-theory preamble.
- Reusable lesson: for an LAA framing, switching and signed adjacency are the
  object, while the period-eight phase is the spectral mechanism.

## SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics

No SIDMA paper was retained. The search did not identify a directly useful
recent SIDMA article that improves on the JGT exhaustive-generation exemplar
for this manuscript's finite proof. Adding a generic algorithmic citation
would dilute the selected library.

## Editorial consequence

The strongest first framing is JGT/EJC: a complete fixed-graph signed
spectral extremal classification, with the G6 phase slip providing the
structural explanation. LAA is a credible alternate framing, but it should
foreground signed adjacency, exact spectral edge, and Floquet operator
language rather than the finite classification alone.
