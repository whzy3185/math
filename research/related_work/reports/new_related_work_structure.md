# Two Related-Work Framings

No manuscript text is changed by this report.

## Framing A: JGT / EJC Priority

**Identity:** complete spectral extremal classification on signed cycle
squares.

1. Introduction paragraph 1: Belardo--Cioaba--Koolen--Wang, then
   Brunetti--Stanic, establish fixed-graph signature minimization and a
   published signed-radius extremal line.
2. Paragraph 2: Hu--Liu supplies the modern non-bipartite Cayley/circulant
   context; Ghorbani--Majidi supports the importance of a fixed graph family.
3. Paragraph 3: only now introduce Suvagiya for flux coordinates, twisted
   candidate, small orders, and the conjecture.
4. Main theorem: immediately after the nonmonotone truth pattern.
5. Structural mechanism: Korotyaev--Saburova and Kuchment--Vainberg frame the
   periodic bulk and localized phase slip.
6. Finite completion: Lin--Ning and Goedgebeur--Schaudt frame the analytic
   tail plus certified finite closure.

Demote Davis, Fredricksen--Maiorana, Lam, the Kuchment book, and the broad
K2-Hamiltonian architecture reference.

## Framing B: LAA Priority

**Identity:** spectral-radius minimization for a structured signed adjacency
family.

1. Introduction paragraph 1: signed adjacency, switching, and fixed-graph
   spectral radius; cite Belardo et al. and Brunetti--Stanic.
2. Paragraph 2: signed complete graph adjacency extrema through
   Ghorbani--Majidi, then distinguish the sparse circulant family.
3. Paragraph 3: period-eight reference phase and the exact local edge;
   cite Korotyaev--Saburova for periodic magnetic/Floquet context.
4. Paragraph 4: introduce Suvagiya's candidate and conjecture.
5. Main theorem: early, followed by operator/defect mechanism.
6. Finite completion: lower in the paper, with computation described as
   closing a spectral classification.

## Recommendation

Framing A is the preferred first submission strategy. It best foregrounds the
all-order classification, nonmonotone truth set, sharp onset, and
local-to-global phase-slip mechanism. Framing B is a defensible alternate if
the paper is recast more strongly around signed adjacency and Floquet spectral
analysis.
