# Vertex Isoperimetry on Signed Graphs and Spectra of Non-Bipartite Cayley Graphs

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Chunyang Hu, Shiping Liu
- Venue/year: European Journal of Combinatorics, 130, Article 104200, 2025
- DOI: 10.1016/j.ejc.2025.104200
- Accessible source: arXiv 2306.05306 author manuscript
- Library role: A2

## Problem

The paper gives signed-graph isoperimetric constants and obtains spectral
interval estimates for non-bipartite finite Cayley, vertex-transitive, and
Cayley-sum graphs.

## Main Theorem Architecture

Signed isoperimetric definitions -> Cheeger-type inequalities -> Cayley and
vertex-transitive spectral consequences -> extensions.

## Proof Architecture

Variational/isoperimetric estimates and group-structured graph analysis, not
signing optimization.

## Reusable Writing

It is a recent EJC signal that non-bipartite Cayley spectral questions and
signed-graph machinery belong in the same serious modern context. Cite only
for this structural context.

## Relation to the Present Paper

Non-bipartite Cayley structural analogue. The paper does not vary edge
signings on a fixed circulant or identify a minimizer.
