# Interlacing Families I: Bipartite Ramanujan Graphs of All Degrees

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Adam W. Marcus, Daniel A. Spielman, Nikhil Srivastava
- Venue/year: Annals of Mathematics, 182(1), 307-325, 2015
- DOI: 10.4007/annals.2015.182.1.7
- Accessible source: arXiv 1304.4132 author manuscript

## Problem and Architecture

The paper proves an interlacing-family existence theorem for signings in the
bipartite setting and turns one-sided control into two-sided control through
spectral symmetry.

## Reusable Writing

The manuscript's non-bipartite setting should be contrasted with this
bipartite symmetry point in one sentence. The present exact optimizer problem
is deliberately different from an existence theorem.

## Relation and Recommendation

Historical benchmark. MUST CITE adjacent to Bilu--Linial when explaining the
one-sided/two-sided distinction.
