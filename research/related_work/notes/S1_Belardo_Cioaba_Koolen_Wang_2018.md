# Open Problems in the Spectral Theory of Signed Graphs

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Francesco Belardo, Sebastian M. Cioaba, Jack H. Koolen, Jianfeng Wang
- Venue/year: The Art of Discrete and Applied Mathematics, 2018

- Status: published; local open-access version of record

## Problem

This survey formulates, among other questions, the fixed connected graph
problem of choosing a signature to minimize adjacency spectral radius.

## Main Theorem Architecture

There is no single classification theorem. The reusable architecture is
problem-first: define the signing invariant, state the extremal question, and
separate it from general signed-graph background.

## Proof Architecture

Survey synthesis: switching, signings, eigenvalues, and open-problem
formulation rather than a proof route for the present theorem.

## Reusable Writing

Define the fixed-graph signature minimization problem before narrowing to
cycle squares. State that the present paper gives one complete instance, not
a solution of the general problem.

## Relation to the Present Paper

Published problem-level predecessor. It supports the importance of the
optimization question, but is not the source of the specific C_n(1,2)
conjecture.
