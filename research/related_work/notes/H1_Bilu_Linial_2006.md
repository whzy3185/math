# Lifts, Discrepancy and Nearly Optimal Spectral Gap

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Yonatan Bilu, Nathan Linial
- Venue/year: Combinatorica, 26(5), 495-519, 2006
- DOI: 10.1007/s00493-006-0029-7
- Accessible source: arXiv math/0312022 author manuscript

## Problem and Architecture

The paper connects signings of a fixed graph to the new eigenvalues of a
two-lift, then seeks broadly controlled signed spectra for regular graphs.
Its architecture is general program -> signing interpretation -> quantitative
bound -> conjectural sharp target.

## Reusable Writing

Use one compact sentence for the origin of the modern signing program. Do not
attribute fixed-family minimizer classification or a two-sided result on
non-bipartite graphs to this paper.

## Relation and Recommendation

Historical motivation. MUST CITE only if the introduction keeps the 2-lift
context.
