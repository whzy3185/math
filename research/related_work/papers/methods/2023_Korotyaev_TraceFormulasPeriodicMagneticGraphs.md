# Trace Formulas for Magnetic Schrodinger Operators on Periodic Graphs

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Evgeny Korotyaev, Natalia Saburova
- Venue/year: Linear Algebra and its Applications, 676, 395-440, 2023
- DOI: 10.1016/j.laa.2023.07.025
- Accessible source: arXiv 2206.09663 author manuscript
- Library role: S3

## Problem

The paper studies periodic magnetic discrete-graph operators. It expresses
fiber traces through quasimomentum Fourier series whose coefficients encode
magnetic fluxes, potentials, and quotient-graph cycles.

## Main Theorem Architecture

Periodic operator definition -> Floquet fiber reduction -> trace formulas ->
bandwidth estimates and sharpness examples.

## Proof Architecture

Floquet decomposition, quotient graph cycle data, finite fiber matrices, and
exact trace identities.

## Reusable Writing

It validates a compact method paragraph: fluxes naturally enter periodic graph
spectra through finite Floquet fibers. Do not imply that it contains the G6
interface theorem or a signing-minimization result.

## Relation to the Present Paper

Method precedent for the reference phase, fiber language, and flux-sensitive
periodic spectral analysis.
