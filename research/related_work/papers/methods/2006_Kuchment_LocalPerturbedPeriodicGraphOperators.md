# Locally Perturbed Periodic Graph Operators

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Peter Kuchment, Boris Vainberg
- Venue/year: Communications in Mathematical Physics, 268(3), 673-686, 2006
- DOI: 10.1007/s00220-006-0105-2
- Accessible source: arXiv math-ph/0511084 author manuscript
- Library role: A5

## Problem

The paper analyzes eigenfunctions at embedded eigenvalues of locally
perturbed periodic graph operators and describes localization near the
perturbation under a Floquet-surface hypothesis.

## Main Theorem Architecture

Periodic operator and local perturbation -> Floquet algebraic condition ->
support/localization structure of eigenfunctions.

## Proof Architecture

Floquet transform, local perturbation, spectral geometry, and eigenfunction
localization.

## Reusable Writing

It supports the conceptual bridge from a periodic bulk to a local interface
state. The present paper's finite-range one-dimensional proof remains
self-contained and should not be represented as an application of this work.

## Relation to the Present Paper

Method precedent for local defect states on periodic graph operators.
