## Problem

The paper gives a proof-producing generator for a finite graph-class
classification, with canonical construction and completeness reasoning.

## Main Theorem Architecture

Define target class -> extension/reduction theorem -> exhaustive generator ->
canonicalization and termination -> classified output.

## Proof Architecture

Structural reductions justify a finite generation process; computation is a
consequence of a completeness theorem rather than a sampled search.
