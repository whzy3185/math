## Problem

The paper optimizes extremal adjacency eigenvalues in complete signed graphs
under constraints on the negative-edge subgraph.

## Main Theorem Architecture

The published abstract and article outline indicate a sequence of extremal
characterizations: constrained maximum-index results, then minimum-eigenvalue
results and equality structures.

## Proof Architecture
