## Problem

The paper completes the limit-point side of the signed-graph Hoffman program
for adjacency spectral radius.

## Main Theorem Architecture

Define the signed spectral-radius program -> identify candidate limit-point
families -> prove completeness of the list -> connect to hereditary small
spectral-radius classes.

## Proof Architecture

Infinite graph families, induced-subgraph monotonicity, spectral recurrence,
and classification of limiting behavior.

## Reusable Writing

It is the best modern published source for presenting signed spectral radius
as a coherent program rather than an isolated construction. It belongs after,
not before, the fixed-graph problem formulation.

## Relation to the Present Paper

Published spectral-radius program analogue; no fixed C_n(1,2) overlap.
