# Unbalanced Signed Graphs with Extremal Spectral Radius or Index

## Bibliographic Information

- Authors: Maurizio Brunetti, Zoran Stanic
- Venue/year: Computational and Applied Mathematics, 41(3), Article 118, 2022
- DOI: 10.1007/s40314-022-01814-5
- Status: published; local open-access version of record
- Library role: S2

## Problem

The paper determines extremal spectral-radius and index values over connected
unbalanced signed graphs of a fixed order, while allowing the underlying graph
to vary.

## Main Theorem Architecture

Class-wide extremal statements are organized by the quantity being optimized:
maximum/minimum spectral radius and maximum/minimum index, followed by
switching characterization of extremizers.

## Proof Architecture

Signed adjacency comparison, structural graph reduction, and explicit
extremal constructions. The setting is global over graph families rather than
a finite-signing quotient of one fixed graph.

## Reusable Writing

Use it to establish that exact signed spectral-radius extremal
classifications are a live published line. Immediately state the crucial
difference: the present paper fixes C_n(1,2) and varies only the signing.

## Relation to the Present Paper

Closest published signed spectral-radius extremal analogue, but not a direct
predecessor of the cycle-square classification.
