# Final article architecture after authorial rewrite

The manuscript now has six sections.

1. **Introduction.** Fixed-graph minimization -> switching-invariant cycle
   data -> cycle-square geometry -> reference edge 8 -> exact period-eight
   phase -> general chiral mechanism -> first occurrence -> rigidity.
2. **Switching coordinates and periodic fibers.** Hamilton gauge, separate
   local flux/global holonomy, lift and dihedral invariance, primitive period,
   finite Bloch sum.
3. **Half-cell chiral symmetry.** Monomial operator iff, flux iff, dimension
   halving, and the period-two limitation that motivates spectral effectiveness.
4. **The exact period-eight phase.** Explicit fiber, `8 -> 4 -> 2`, human-
   checkable quartic, four bands, both holonomies, twisted comparison.
5. **First occurrence, rigidity, and defect constraints.** Local square,
   transparent `M3`, complete cyclic-gap reduction, certificates, recurrence,
   trichotomy, and the general necessary obstruction.
6. **Concluding remarks.** One precise global-minimum/equality-class problem.

The former Section 6 was merged into Section 5 because it repeated the moments
already needed for minimal period.  General chiral symmetry remains independent
because it is a reusable iff rather than a period-eight calculation.
