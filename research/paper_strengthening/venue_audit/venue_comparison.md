## Current theorem package being evaluated

The venue decision is based only on the strengthened, verified package:

1. exact positive-holonomy finite spectral radius for every (L\ge1);
2. strict comparison with the twisted signing for every (L\ge4);
3. exact negative-holonomy finite formula;
4. no legal period below eight has squared Bloch edge below eight;
5. uniqueness of the sub-eight period-eight class modulo natural symmetries;
6. general (M_1,M_2,M_3) defect-density and clustering obstructions;

classification of arbitrary unitary chiral involutions, or an all-period
classification.

## Ranking

### Mathematical fit

1. **LAA**: the strongest new content is exact finite matrix spectral information.
2. **JGT**: the minimal-period and period-eight rigidity theorems create a real graph-theoretic story.
3. **SIGMA**: the general-mechanism gate now passes, but the direct-literature
   and community-interest gates remain weak.  It is no longer ruled out on
   theorem form alone, but it is not the recommended submission.

### Strategic recommendation

If the authors prefer the originally stated ambition, submit first to **JGT**, but build the manuscript so it can be converted to LAA without changing the proof.  This is reasonable because the JGT version now has more than a single counterexample: it explains the first possible period and the unique period-eight flux geometry.

If minimizing venue-risk is more important than preserving the original target, submit first to **LAA**.  The exact dispersion and both holonomy formulas are immediately legible as finite-dimensional spectral results.
