# Post-theorem discussion map

| major result | proved | interpretation/comparison | limitation | natural continuation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| exact finite radius | the positive-holonomy ring attains `eta` for every `L>=1`; beats twisted for `L>=4` | finite attainment is uniform in cell count, not an asymptotic estimate | good witness does not explain solvability | inspect `tau_(i+4)=-tau_i` |
| half-cell criterion | monomial anticommutation iff half-antiperiodic `tau`, equivalently half-periodic `Q` with negative half flux | operator symmetry is readable from switching-invariant cycle data | period-two is chiral but has edge 8 | ask when chirality first crosses below 8 |
| full dispersion | four branches and gaps follow from the centered quartic | disappearance of odd powers after `y=X+4` is extra algebra beyond generic chirality; holonomies sample one law on different grids | exact solution does not prove first occurrence or uniqueness | turn to defect geometry |
| minimal period | no primitive period below eight is sub-eight | chirality appears earlier; spectral effectiveness starts at eight | says nothing about multiplicity of first-period phases | classify period-eight survivors |
| rigidity | one dihedral `Q` orbit is sub-eight; lift choice is separate | survivor has two maximally separated defects, compatible with density/clustering penalties | compatibility is not all-period sufficiency | return to arbitrary-period necessary bounds |
| general obstruction | density and near-clustering inequalities hold for every low-edge periodic word | identifies general local geometry | does not determine global minimum or all long periods | state the fixed-graph minimization problem in the conclusion |
