# JGT reviewer simulation

## Reviewer A: spectral graph theory

**Assessment.** The revised opening now begins with the fixed-support extremal
problem and explains why the cycle square is selected: Fourier structure comes
from the cyclic backbone, while overlapping triangles localize switching
invariants.  The threshold eight is supplied by the alternating period-two
phase before “sub-eight” is used.  The general flux criterion prevents the
paper from reading as one special matrix calculation.

**Substantive concern found and repaired.** The earlier Introduction used a
neighboring signed Cayley citation only to broaden coverage.  It did not advance
the problem chain and has been removed from the manuscript core.

**Residual judgment.** JGT fit is credible.  The paper solves a restricted
periodic problem rather than the global minimum, but first occurrence and
rigidity make that restriction mathematically meaningful.

## Reviewer B: proof skeptic

**Assessment.** The chiral criterion, finite Bloch decomposition, exact finite
attainment, and orbit invariances are transparent.  The branch notation and
`Q`-orbit/lift distinction are now consistent.

**Substantive concerns found and repaired.** Three proof gaps in presentation
were real:

1. `P(y,c)` previously followed from “expansion”; the revised text prints
   `R(xi)Q(xi)`, its trace and determinant, and the scalar quadratic.
2. `M3` previously appeared with unexplained coefficients; the revised text
   gives the four closed-step support classes and converts them algebraically
   to `d,a,b`.
3. the survivor list previously asserted completeness; the revised proof uses
   parity, the density bound, cyclic gap compositions, the `(d,a,b)` column,
   and the dihedral quotient.

The recurrence is now a lemma with separation, first decisive `k`, and exact
excess paired in one statement.  The band gaps have explicit positive
separations.

**Residual judgment.** A referee can check the mathematical proof from the
paper alone.  Independent scripts remain useful regression tests but are no
longer hidden proof steps.

## Reviewer C: exposition and style

**Assessment.** The Introduction is a continuous problem chain rather than a
textbook opening plus theorem inventory.  Major results are separated by
interpretation and a mathematical limitation.  Optional bracket titles have
been removed from theorem, lemma, proposition, and proof headings.

**Substantive concerns found and repaired.** The independent defect-obstruction
section repeated the moment identities, so it was merged into the first-
occurrence section.  The box-and-arrow chiral figure repeated equations without
adding understanding and was removed.  Figure 1 had reversed half-cell labels;
the labels now agree with vertices `0,1,2,3` and `4,5,6,7`, and chord style
shows the triangle-flux signs.

**Residual judgment.** Connector density is substantially reduced.  Remaining
phrases such as “conversely” and “consequently” perform local proof work rather
than project navigation.  The prose is formal without manufactured
informality.
