# Bounded source check for the general Hall statements

Checked on 5 October 2026.

The exact statements checked for possible prior overlap were:

- χ_f(G)<3 implies ρ(μ(G))≤3
- χ_f(G)<3 implies ρ(μ²(G))≤10/3
- The specialization ρ(M6)=10/3, with M2=K2 and |M6|=47

## Primary material inspected

1. Cropper, Gyárfás and Lehel, *Hall ratio of the Mycielski graphs*, Discrete Mathematics306 (2006),1988–1990. The primary three-page paper gives the known values through M5 and an unboundedness result. Its page1989 gives the classical fractional values29/10 and941/290. No statement of the specific general one-step/two-step pair above was located in this paper.
   - https://users.renyi.hu/~gyarfas/Cikkek/114_hallratio.pdf
   - https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2005.09.020

2. Johnathan B. Barnett, *The Fractional Chromatic Number and the Hall Ratio*, Auburn dissertation,2016. Chapter4 was directly reread from printed pages22–28. It presents the Mycielski construction, standard chromatic and fractional-chromatic facts, vertex counts, and the whole-graph independence result. It ends with the remaining Hall-profile research problem. No equivalent statement of the specific χ_f<3 one-step/two-step bounds was located in that chapter. Definition2.1.1 establishes the pre-existing profile notation w(a,G).
   - https://etd.auburn.edu/bitstream/handle/10415/5316/Final%20Dissertation%20-%20Johnathan%20Barnett.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2

3. Michael Larsen, James Propp and Daniel Ullman, *The fractional chromatic number of Mycielski's graphs*, Journal of Graph Theory19(3) (1995),411–416. This is cited for the established fractional-coloring recurrence. The primary metadata and abstract were checked in the independent audit; this is not presented as a full-text overlap audit for the Hall statements.
   - https://doi.org/10.1002/jgt.3190190313

4. Raphael Steiner, *Fractional Chromatic Number Vs. Hall Ratio*, Combinatorica45:37 (2025). The introduction still identifies boundedness of χ_f(Mr)/ρ(Mr) as open. That broader question is distinct from the finite-step result here.
   - https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00164-0

5. Tsukiyama, Ide, Ariyoshi and Shirakawa, SIAM Journal on Computing6(3) (1977),505–517. The primary record gives established output-sensitive maximal-independent-set enumeration. The present work makes no general enumeration-improvement claim.
   - https://doi.org/10.1137/0206036

## Search scope and limit

Targeted searches combined Hall ratio, Mycielskian/Mycielski, fractional chromatic number below3, double Mycielski,10/3, and related Stiebitz/Steiner references. No verified matching primary statement was located. The source check is bounded and is not an exhaustive bibliographic review or publication-priority clearance.

The manuscript therefore says that it proves the stated bounds. It does not claim they are the first such bounds, that the threshold3 is necessary, or that the asymptotic Mycielski problem is solved.
