## Version 2

*Sharp edge bounds for bipartite graphs with unique minimum domination* is a coherent expansion of the same paper, stored here separately. It adds:

1. Complete first-boundary equality classification at γ=2,3, with prior finite-case overlap acknowledged
2. The sharp second-boundary maximum ⌈γ²/2⌉+5γ
3. Two nonisomorphic connected second-boundary extremals for even γ≥4
4. Shared local lemmas and a unified construction for the two regimes
5. Exact comparison with the prior 14-vertex certificate and unbalanced q=1 family

It preserves the definitions, original proof, source attribution and audit boundaries. It does not assert a complete second-boundary equality classification. Its mathematical components passed independent review; the final integrated manuscript also passed a separate review, recorded in audit/FINAL_INTEGRATED_REVIEW.md.

The old PDF and its published identity are not overwritten by this directory. Publication of this revision is recorded separately by its final release manifest.
