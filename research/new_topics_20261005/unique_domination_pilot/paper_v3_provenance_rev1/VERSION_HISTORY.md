It adds the complete second-boundary3/1/2 equality catalog, the first-boundary bound min{γ(γ+7)−t,12γt} for edge-edit distance under arbitrary vertex bijections, and connected examples establishing the matching order and failure of dense asymptotic edit stability. These corrections are incorporated in the final source.

The old PDF bytes and their publication identities are preserved. Release records for this revision are maintained separately; this file does not claim publication before it occurs.
