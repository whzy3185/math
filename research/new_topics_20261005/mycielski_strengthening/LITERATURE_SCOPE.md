# Literature scope and novelty limits

Checked on 5 October 2026.

The convention here is M2=K2, M3=C5, so M6 has 47 vertices. Numbering conventions differ in some literature.

## Verified primary context

- Cropper, Gyárfás, Lehel, *Hall ratio of the Mycielski graphs*, Discrete Mathematics 306 (2006), 1988–1990. Primary PDF: https://users.renyi.hu/~gyarfas/Cikkek/114_hallratio.pdf ; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2005.09.020 . This gives the known values through M5 and proves unboundedness of the sequence.
- Barnett, 2016 dissertation, Definition 2.1.1 and Chapter 4: https://etd.auburn.edu/bitstream/handle/10415/5316/Final%20Dissertation%20-%20Johnathan%20Barnett.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2 . The Hall-profile formulation w(a,G) predates the present calculation.
- Steiner, *Fractional Chromatic Number Vs. Hall Ratio*, Combinatorica 45:37 (2025): https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00164-0 . Its introduction identifies boundedness of chi_f/rho for iterated Mycielskians as open. A finite value and extremizer classification do not settle that asymptotic question.

## Targeted search

Searches combining Mycielski, Hall ratio, 10/3, M6, 47, and 3.333 did not locate a primary report of this exact M6 value or classification. This is a bounded negative search, not exhaustive novelty certification.

Additional searches for maximal independent sets of Mycielskians located prior work on whole-graph independence numbers. The primary publisher search result for *The exponential growth of the packing chromatic number of iterated Mycielskians* explicitly cites earlier independence formulas: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0166218X23003098 . The full publisher page could not be opened in this pass (HTTP 403), so it is not treated as a completed full-text priority audit of the neighborhood-closure theorem.

Accordingly, this package claims proved identities, an exact reproducible finite value, and an exact finite extremizer classification. It does not claim that the general identities, profile formulation, value, or classification are publication-new.
