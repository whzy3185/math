# Bounded literature check for the circulant extension

Checked on 2026-09-05. This is a targeted comparison, not a systematic
review or a certificate of priority. The frozen manuscript bibliography
has not been changed.

classification. Short topic queries were used; no manuscript or full proof
was uploaded to another model.

## Current novelty disposition

* **Known framework:** switching, fixed-graph signing minimization,
  chiral block decomposition, path continuants and Schur elimination.
* **Strong known-overlap warning:** flat `A^2=4I` constructions and their
  general classification. The direct parameter and equality analysis is
  useful internally but not yet established as an original main result.
* **No directly matching result located in this search:** the specified
  primitive period-`4s` antipodal word, its uniform sub-eight proof for
  every even jump, its eight-endpoint formula and the explicit polynomial
  finite-comparison threshold.
* **Unresolved:** priority of that construction under another graph or
  operator description. Magnetic lattice, periodic Jacobi/strip and
  defect-state sources still need comparison before a novelty claim.

The proof establishes the stated mathematics regardless of this limited
search result; publication novelty is a separate question. In particular,
absence of a hit is not evidence that no prior theorem implies the result.
