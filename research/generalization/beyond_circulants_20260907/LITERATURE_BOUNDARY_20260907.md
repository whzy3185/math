# Literature boundary for the beyond-circulants extension

Date: 2026-09-07.

This is a positioning note, not a priority certificate.

## Strong known overlap

1. **Huang-type hypercube signing.**  The signed hypercube with square
   `A^2=dI` is classical in the sensitivity-theorem literature and predates
   this workstream.  Our `m(Q_d)=sqrt(d)` corollary is a recovery/application,
   not a novelty claim.
2. **Signed Cartesian products.**  There is established literature on spectra
   and algebra of Cartesian products of signed graphs, including product
   constructions producing few-eigenvalue signed graphs.
3. **Signed toral tessellations.**  Z. Stanic (Discrete Mathematics Letters
   17 (2026), 70--74) studies signed toral tessellations with exactly two
   symmetric eigenvalues, explicitly extending known 4-regular toral
   tessellations with eigenvalues `+-2`.
4. **Two-eigenvalue signed graphs.**  Broader classification and construction
   literature already exists, especially in small degree.

## Current contribution boundary

The safe mathematical content of this directory is:

- one compact anticommutation theorem covering arbitrary bipartite factors;
- the exact square-spectrum identity and minimum-radius product inequality;
- the closure theorem: Cartesian products of bipartite regular flat-signing
  factors again attain the universal trace lower bound;
- integration of this product mechanism with the existing circulant
  arithmetic program;
- a structural square-lattice quotient model for non-unit two-generator
  circulants.

Before manuscript novelty wording, the product theorem should be compared
line-by-line with existing signed-product and two-eigenvalue constructions.
The repository currently treats it as an analytic extension framework, not a
priority claim.

## Primary comparison targets

- Zoran Stanic, *Signed Toral Tessellations Whose Spectrum Consists of Exactly
  Two Symmetric Eigenvalues*, Discrete Math. Lett. 17 (2026), 70--74,
  DOI 10.47443/dml.2025.225.
- Germina, Hameed, Zaslavsky, work on products and line graphs of signed
  graphs (signed Cartesian/NEPS spectral formulas).
- Huang's signed hypercube construction and subsequent product
  generalizations motivated by the sensitivity theorem.
- General signed-graph two-eigenvalue literature and the 2026 monograph
  *Spectra of Signed Graphs*.
