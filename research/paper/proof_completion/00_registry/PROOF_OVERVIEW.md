# Canonical Registry Proof Overview

## Purpose

The registry is valid if it satisfies four conditions:

1. every accepted final theorem has one stable claim ID;
2. every claim has one exact evidence label and scope;
3. every mathematical dependency can be followed to analytic leaves or
   explicit finite exact lemmas;
4. every known open, read-only, producer-only, or falsified statement is kept
   outside the accepted theorem set.

## Construction

### Mathematical reduction

The accepted corpus is organized by mathematical subject, not chronology:

```text
signed graph and switching
-> reference phase
-> gaps, charge, and sectors
-> G6 phase slip
-> single-gap hierarchy
-> separated slips and IMS
-> residue constructions
-> complete even-order classification.
```

Supporting moments, bounded periodic results, and finite exact lemmas receive
the separate prefixes `A` and `C`.

### Finite exact objects

The logically essential finite objects are isolated as `C.1`-`C.9`. They
include finite signing quotients, exact rational matrices, a parity-lifted
state graph, exact polynomial/root objects, Grassmann chart coverage,
integer Rayleigh rows, and exact Floquet interval bounds.

### Machine verification

The [evidence matrix](../TARGET_A_FINAL_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX_V2.md) names a
producer, independent checker, and certificate whenever machine assistance is
essential. Analytic deductions say `N/A`; they do not borrow a machine label
merely because a corroborative test exists.

### Consequence

The [dependency graph](../TARGET_A_FINAL_PROOF_DEPENDENCY_GRAPH.md) shows that
the main classification `T8.4` depends only on the finite classifications
`T8.1`-`T8.3` and the explicit tail `T7.3`. Those nodes in turn close at
analytic identities or explicit `C.x` lemmas.

## Critical Corrections Preserved

The registry treats the following as permanent corrections:

```text
rank H_6(c6)=2, not 1;
exact 2r, not exact r;
codimension 2r, not codimension r;
2r x 2r Feshbach, not r x r;
sigma_sec(q)=q mod 4, not q/2 mod 4.
```

## Boundary

The registry excludes universal multi-gap optimality, unrestricted common
liminf/limit claims, all-period classification, general interaction
coefficients, and period-25/26 promotion. These exclusions are logical parts
of the package, not missing editorial work.
