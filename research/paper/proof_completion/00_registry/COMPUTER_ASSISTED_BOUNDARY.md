# Canonical Registry Computer-Assisted Boundary

## What The Registry Itself Uses

The act of assigning IDs, labels, dependencies, and editorial placements does
not require a computer-assisted mathematical proof. Link validation and
consistency scans are editorial checks only.

The registered mathematical corpus does contain logically essential
computer-assisted lemmas. They are isolated as `C.1`-`C.9` in the
[inventory](../TARGET_A_FINAL_CLAIM_INVENTORY_V2.md).

## Required Four-Part Boundary

For every `COMPUTER_ASSISTED_PROVED` claim, the theorem-specific package must
state all four items below.

1. `MATHEMATICAL REDUCTION`: why the finite object covers every case.
2. `FINITE EXACT OBJECT`: its exact schema and finite domain.
3. `MACHINE VERIFICATION`: arithmetic type and independence mechanism.
4. `MATHEMATICAL CONSEQUENCE`: the theorem that follows from a PASS.

| `C.9` | primitive periodic orbit partition through 24 | exact orbit logic and Gaussian-integer Rayleigh bounds | checker rebuilds Bloch matrices and quotients |

## Floating-Point Boundary

Floating-point eigensolvers may be used only to locate a root or propose an
integer witness. No accepted theorem endpoint is decided by floating point.
The logical endpoints use rational positivity, exact integer Rayleigh
comparison, symbolic determinant identities, Sturm/root isolation, or
certified rational intervals.

## Non-Promoted Computations

- Periods 25 and 26 remain `EXACT_FINITE_READ_ONLY`.
- The reference-relative graph remains `EXACT_FINITE_PRODUCER`.
- High-precision Evans calculations remain corroborative unless bound to a
  finite exact theorem.

None of these objects may be used as a theorem premise in the canonical DAG.

## Rank-Two Safety

Any checker or certificate used by the final package must reject a regression
to `exact r`, codimension `r`, or an `r x r` problem-specific Feshbach model.
The accepted certificate family requires `INDEPENDENT_CHECKER_PASS` and the
correct cluster rank `2r`.
