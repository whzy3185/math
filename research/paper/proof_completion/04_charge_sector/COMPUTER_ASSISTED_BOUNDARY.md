# Computer-Assisted Boundary

## Logical status

**NONE REQUIRED FOR THIS THEOREM.**

Every assertion follows from cyclic counting and congruences. The optional
JSON artifact checks representative words but is not used as a substitute
for the proof.

## Finite exact object

The four sectors are the four translates of the length-four word

```text
(+,-,-,-).
```

The only finite verification is that advancing an endpoint by `g` changes
its residue by `g mod 4`. The general composition law follows by induction,
not by enumeration.

## Scope boundary

The charge law does not prove:

- existence of a localized eigenvalue for a charged interface;
- a spectral ordering among interfaces of the same charge;
- a universal finite-core `B_0 -> B_2` theorem;
- simplicity or noninteraction of several phase slips.

It is a complete combinatorial coordinate theorem, not a spectral theorem.
