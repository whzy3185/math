# Computer-Assisted Boundary: Residue and IMS Package

## Logical boundary

[MAIN_TEXT_REQUIRED]

**NONE REQUIRED FOR THE IMS IDENTITY, PATCH CLASSIFICATION, RESIDUE LEGALITY,
OR LIMSUP DEDUCTION, ONCE THE TWO UPSTREAM SPECTRAL EDGES ARE ACCEPTED.**

The proof of the IMS identity is entrywise algebra. The tent normalization
and remainder are finite sums with rational closed forms. Patch completeness
is a range-four support argument. Gap sums, parity, sector closure, and
diverging separation are integer calculations.

## Upstream machine-assisted inputs

[APPENDIX_REQUIRED]

The exact value and global-edge property of `c6` are computer-assisted. The
proof isolates an algebraic root, proves physical Evans matching, and excludes
all higher physical candidates by exact/interval methods. The present package
uses only the resulting theorem `sup sigma(H_6)=c6`.

The optional exponential estimate is also computer-assisted. Its finite
certificate verifies reciprocal Floquet structure, exact rational interval
bounds, the rank-`2t` count, the codimension-`2t` complement, and the constants
`9/25`, `3505t`, and `D>=1040`.

The qualitative `limsup` theorem does not need the exponential certificate;
the exact IMS cap already tends to zero.

## Arithmetic audits

[REPRODUCIBILITY_ONLY]

The archived IMS/residue certificate checks finite sample words and exact
rational endpoint arithmetic. Those checks are regression evidence, not a
substitute for the all-`k` symbolic proof in `FULL_PROOF.md`.

No floating-point number is used at a logical endpoint. Numerical spectra may
locate candidate witnesses upstream, but every accepted comparison is exact
rational, algebraic, or interval-certified.
