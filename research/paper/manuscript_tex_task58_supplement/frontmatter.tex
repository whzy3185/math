\title{Supplementary Material for\\
Spectral Radius Minimization for Signed Squares of Cycles}
\author{Anonymous}
\date{}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This supplement records two exact components of the proof package.  First,
it proves that a finite signed ring with \(r\in\{1,2,3\}\) mutually separated
elementary six-gap interfaces has exactly \(2r\) squared eigenvalues in a
fixed neighborhood of the one-interface level \(\cSix\), provided the
minimum cyclic separation is at least \(1040\).  The proof includes the
finite-ring patch identification, the full codimension-\(2r\) complement
estimate, and the \(2r\)-dimensional Feshbach equation.  Second, it gives
all integer Rayleigh witnesses used to separate every other abnormal
positive single gap from the six-gap edge by more than \(1/250\).  The final
section identifies the exact repository artifacts and independent checkers
supporting the computer-assisted inputs.  All finite-support comparisons
printed here are checkable by integer arithmetic.
\end{abstract}
