\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains a structural refinement of the interface theory,
the complete single-gap witness data, and the reproducibility record for the
computer-assisted portions of the article. The separated-interface cluster
theorem gives the exact \(2r\)-eigenvalue count near \(\cSix\), but is not
used in the classification proof. The final section distinguishes producer
code, independently reconstructed objects, and shared symbolic routines.
\end{abstract}
