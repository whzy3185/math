\label{sec:g6-raw-enclosures}

This section records the outward rational enclosures whose signs are used in
Appendix A of the article. They are separated from the argument so that the
appendix retains the physical matching, monotonicity, and chart-completeness
logic without becoming a raw certificate listing.
