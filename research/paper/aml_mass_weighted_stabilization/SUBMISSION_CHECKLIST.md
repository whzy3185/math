# AML submission checklist

- Manuscript title frozen.
- Authors and affiliation inserted in `main.tex`.
- Four author emails recorded.
- Four ORCID identifiers recorded in `AUTHOR_METADATA.txt` and LaTeX source comments.
- Funding statement: no external funding.
- Competing-interest statement: none.
- Data statement: no data used.
- Highlights prepared.
- Cover letter prepared.
- Mathematics/proofs frozen unless a definite error is found.
- Final local BibTeX compilation succeeds in `elsarticle` `final,5p,times,twocolumn` format in 4 pages.
- Final September 2026 quick literature search found no exact theorem-level collision; nearest current papers concern Qin--Zheng boundedness, indirect-signal mechanisms, or different reaction/motility regimes.

## Still requiring an author decision in the submission portal

- Designate one corresponding author.
- Enter CRediT author-contribution roles if requested by the portal.
- Confirm whether the portal requests declarations as separate upload items in addition to the statements already included in the manuscript.
