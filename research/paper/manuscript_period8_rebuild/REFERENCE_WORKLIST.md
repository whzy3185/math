## JGT preparation facts verified on 2026-09-04

- JGT describes its scope as graph theory, combinatorics, theoretical graph
  algorithms, and discrete optimization.
- Its author guidance requests numbered in-text citations and a reference list
  alphabetized by first-author surname.
- Submission is through Wiley Authors.  The guidance requests a data
  availability statement; for this theoretical paper, the statement should
  point to the public proof/Lean repository or state what is archived.
