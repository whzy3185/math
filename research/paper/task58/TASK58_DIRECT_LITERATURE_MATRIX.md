## Matrix-Level Conclusions

1. The direct predecessor `arXiv:2607.18334` must be named prominently; it is
   the source of Conjecture 3 and has **HIGH** overlap.
2. The general fixed-graph signature-minimization problem predates Target A.
3. Signed-cycle Fourier spectra, periodic Floquet theory, and flux-dependent
   traces are established tools and are not novelty claims.
4. Complete signed spectral-radius classifications exist when the underlying
   graph is allowed to vary; Target A's novelty must emphasize the fixed
   four-regular circulant family, exact all-order truth set, and elementary
   defect mechanism.
5. No verified item found in this search gives the same all-even-order
   classification for fixed `C_n^2` beyond the direct conjecture preprint.
6. The computer-assisted precedents support the proof architecture, not the
   mathematical novelty.
